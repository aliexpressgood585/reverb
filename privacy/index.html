PRIVACY POLICY

CAIRN does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no login, no server, and no analytics service. The game has no code that sends anything anywhere.

What the game stores, and where

CAIRN remembers your progress so your tower survives closing the app. All of it is written to your device's local storage and never leaves it:

  • the positions of the bodies in your tower, and its random seed
  • your highest climb, death count, and lifetime statistics
  • which marks you have earned, and your daily streak
  • your settings: sound, haptics, language

None of this identifies you. There is no name, no email, no device identifier, and no advertising ID in any of it.

Deleting it

Settings → Erase the tower deletes everything the game has stored, immediately and permanently. Uninstalling the app does the same. Neither requires contacting anyone, because there is nobody holding a second copy.

Permissions

  • Vibration — for the haptic feedback on landing and death. It can be switched off in Settings.
  • Internet — required by the Android system component the game runs inside. CAIRN itself makes no network requests and plays fully offline.

Children

CAIRN is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect data.

Advertising and purchases

The version published today contains no advertising and no purchases. If an optional rewarded video is added in a future release, the advertising provider may process data under its own policy, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the Play listing's Data Safety section will be updated with it. An advertisement will never be required to play.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date below changes with it. There is no mailing list to notify because there are no addresses to notify.
